Privacy Policy – Road QC Calculator

Effective date: 14 August 2026

In summary: Road QC Calculator is designed to work locally on your Android device. It does not require an account and does not intentionally collect, sell, or share personal information with the developer or third parties.

1. About the App

Road QC Calculator is a field quality-control calculation application for road construction and related engineering testing. It provides calculation tools, pass/fail evaluation, test records and report-generation functionality.

2. Information We Collect

Road QC Calculator does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use its core functionality.

Information entered by the user for calculations and reports is processed locally on the device. We do not intentionally transmit this information to a developer-operated server.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

The application may store information locally to support its functionality, including test and calculation inputs, departmental information, Tested By and Checked By information, and application settings.

This information remains under the user's control on the device and is not intentionally uploaded to the developer's servers.

4. How Information Is Used

Information entered into the application is used only to provide requested application functions, including calculations, pass/fail evaluation, display of results and generation of reports.

5. Data Sharing

We do not intentionally sell, rent, or share user-entered information with third parties. The application does not intentionally transmit user-entered test information, reports, or personal information to external servers operated by the developer.

6. Advertising and Analytics

The current version of Road QC Calculator does not contain advertising and does not intentionally use third-party advertising or analytics services to collect information about users.

7. Permissions and Device Information

The application is designed without requiring access to sensitive device capabilities such as contacts, microphone, camera, location, SMS, or phone functions. Normal Android operating-system services may be involved in installing, running, printing, or providing standard Android functionality.

8. Reports and Printing

Reports generated by Road QC Calculator are created for the user's use. If a user chooses to print, share, or export a report using Android or another application, handling after it leaves Road QC Calculator is governed by the service or application selected by the user.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored by the application remains on the user's device until deleted through available application functions, application-data clearing, or uninstallation, as applicable. We do not maintain a developer-operated server containing the user's locally stored test information.

10. Children's Privacy

Road QC Calculator is a professional engineering and road-quality-control tool and is not directed toward children. The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Security

We aim to keep the application and its handling of information consistent with applicable platform security requirements. Users should also protect access to their Android device and any exported or shared reports.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data-handling practices change. The updated policy will be made available at the privacy-policy URL associated with Road QC Calculator.
